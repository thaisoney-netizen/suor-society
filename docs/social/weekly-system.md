# The weekly system — 6 hours, survivable on a bad week

The point of this file: **"what do I post?" is never a daily question.** It's
answered once, on Sunday, from menus that already exist.

## The week at a glance

| When | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunday | Batch session — plan, design, caption, schedule the whole week | 2.5–3 h |
| During workouts | Capture b-roll (shot list below) — no extra session, ever | 0 h |
| Mon–Fri, daily | Engagement block: replies + 10 outbound comments | 15 min/day ≈ 1.25 h |
| Friday | Scorecard + pick next week's Take candidates from the Notion board | 30 min |
| **Total** | | **≈ 5–5.5 h** |

## Sunday batch session (2.5–3 h)

Run it as a checklist, same order every week:

1. **Pick (20 min).** Open the trend-radar Notion board → choose 1–2 Take
   candidates (the radar already suggests format + angle per candidate).
   Pick 1–2 hooks from the evergreen library (template kit). Pick the week's
   Race Intel topic and check if any gear dropped. Slot into the weekly grid
   (below).
2. **Design (60–75 min).** Fill the Canva templates — carousel, static,
   gear card. Templates start 80% done; you're changing words and one image.
3. **Cut reels (45–60 min).** Assemble the week's b-roll in CapCut/IG, drop
   the hook text on, trending-or-quiet audio. A hook reel is 7–15 seconds —
   two per week is four minutes of finished video.
4. **Captions + schedule (30 min).** Caption skeletons are in the template
   kit. Schedule everything in Meta Business Suite (IG) + TikTok scheduler +
   LinkedIn scheduler. The week is now done before it starts.

## The default weekly grid

| Day | Instagram | TikTok | LinkedIn (Suor page) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Hook reel (Real-Life Hybrid) | same reel, native caption | — |
| Tue | Take carousel | carousel as photo post | carousel as PDF document post |
| Wed | *engagement only* | — | — |
| Thu | Race Intel carousel or Gear card | reel #2 cross-post | article link post w/ 2-sentence intro |
| Fri | Dispatch teaser (T6) + brand static in stories | — | — |
| Sat | Story: raw b-roll from the run | — | — |

Swap days freely; keep the *counts*: IG 4 feed posts + ~3 stories, TikTok 2–3
cross-posts, LinkedIn 2. On a catastrophic week, the minimum viable week is
**one hook reel + the Friday Dispatch teaser** — never zero, never an apology
post.

## Daily 15 minutes (this is where a small account actually grows)

At <1K followers, comments convert better than content. Timer on, then out:

- Reply to every comment and DM on your posts (always, same day).
- Leave **10 genuinely good comments** — funny or additive, in the house
  voice, never "Great post!" — on: hybrid/HYROX athletes, run-club and
  running-culture meme accounts, San Diego local running accounts, Brazilian
  runners posting in English. Suor's deadpan in other people's comment
  sections is free distribution to exactly the right audience.
- React to a few stories from target accounts (opens DM relationships).

## Friday scorecard (10 min) + radar pass (20 min)

Copy this row into a running sheet (or the Notion board) every Friday:

| Week | Posts shipped | Reach | Profile visits | Link taps | Follows | Dispatch signups (social) | Best post | Worst post | One lesson |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Sources: IG Professional Dashboard, TikTok Analytics, LinkedIn page analytics,
Buttondown/GA4 (`generate_lead` events) for signups. Then skim the week's
radar candidates on the Notion board and star next week's Takes.

## Monthly review (last Friday, +30 min)

- Sort the month's posts by reach and by saves. **Double the winning pillar's
  slot, halve the losing one's.** The grid is a starting allocation, not law.
- Check the PT signal: if Brazil-flavored posts are consistently top-quartile,
  that's the trigger to plan the pt-br sister account.
- Screenshot the dashboard. That's a portfolio artifact — file it.

## B-roll shot list (capture during training, 10–20 s each)

Keep ~15 clips banked; grab 3–5 per week while already out:

1. Shoes hitting pavement (low angle, side-on)
2. Lacing up / chalk on hands / gripping the bar
3. Sunrise or golden hour on the run route
4. San Diego texture — palms, coastline, the street the crew will run
5. Watch close-up starting a run
6. Barbell/kettlebell mid-set (tripod against a wall, face optional)
7. Water fountain, sweat on the ground, the "after" mess
8. POV walking into the gym / out the front door pre-dawn
9. Race-adjacent anything: bibs, medals, expo, spectators
10. Slow pan over gear laid out for tomorrow

Every hook in the library pairs with any of these. That's the trick: hooks
and footage are interchangeable parts, so a reel is assembly, not creation.

## Rules that keep the system alive

1. **Never miss twice.** One skipped post is noise; two in a row is a trend.
2. **Batch is sacred, timing is not.** Posting Tuesday 9am vs 7pm doesn't
   matter at this size. The streak matters.
3. **Don't check numbers between Fridays.** Mid-week analytics is how the
   void wins.
4. **When in doubt, ship Pillar 5.** Brand energy is the proven performer
   and the cheapest to make.
