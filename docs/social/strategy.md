# Strategy — what the account is, who it's for, why it wins

## Positioning

**The hybrid running culture page for people who train around a real life.**

Not a coach (no programs, no form checks). Not a news wire (no reposts without
a take). An *editorial voice* — the friend with great taste who lifts, runs,
reads everything, and tells you what actually matters. The IKEA-marathon
energy: "the smartest dumb race of 2026" is the house voice.

Three things make @suorsociety distinct in a feed full of identical hybrid
accounts:

1. **Editorial deadpan.** Takes, verdicts, and jokes — in on the joke, never
   above it (this is already codified in the trend-radar filter).
2. **The Brazilian thread.** *Suor* is Portuguese for sweat. Occasional PT
   hooks, Brazilian race culture (São Silvestre, HYROX Brasil), the
   "brasileira in San Diego" texture. Flavor, not a second content pipeline —
   English-first until PT content visibly outperforms, then a pt-br sister
   account becomes a data-backed decision.
3. **The real-life frame.** The 1:10 half took eight years. Training around
   kids, work, everything else. This is the emotional core and the About page
   already says it perfectly.

## Audience

25–40, lifts AND runs (or wants to), has a job/kids/life, follows fitness
content but is tired of full-time-athlete advice. Two overlapping circles:
the global hybrid/HYROX-curious crowd, and San Diego locals (future crew
recruits). They follow accounts that make them feel *seen and smart*, and
they save posts that are useful later.

## The 5 content pillars

Every post belongs to exactly one pillar. If an idea fits none, skip it —
skipping without guilt is already the trend-radar rule.

| # | Pillar | What it is | Format | Feed source | Weekly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The Take** | Running/hybrid news retold with a verdict — IKEA marathon, Cape Town major, HYROX calendar | Take carousel (T1) or hook reel (T2) | Trend radar → Notion board, daily | 1–2 |
| 2 | **Real-Life Hybrid** | Relatable identity content — the feelings of training around a life | Hook reel (T2) over training b-roll | Evergreen hook library (template kit) | 1–2 |
| 3 | **Race Intel** | Useful, save-able race info — open-entry lists, HYROX dates, race picks | Intel carousel (T3) | `src/content/races-*.json`, existing guides | 1 |
| 4 | **Gear Verdict** | Drops with a "who is this actually for" call | Gear card (T4) or hook reel | Trend radar + monthly shoe-drop dispatches | 2–3/month |
| 5 | **Suor World** | Pure brand: flag energy, suor = sweat, San Diego scenes, PT moments | Brand static (T5) | Brand asset shot list | 1 |

Pillar 5 is the proven top performer (1,001 views) — never let it get crowded
out by "useful" content. It's what makes people follow rather than just save.

## Platform roles

**Instagram — home base.** 4 feed posts/week (2 reels, 1 carousel, 1 static)
plus stories ~3x/week (raw b-roll, polls, post amplification). All six
templates live here.

**TikTok — distribution, not a second job.** Cross-post the 2 reels/week with
TikTok-native captions (more casual, hook in first line). Carousels also
cross-post as TikTok photo posts — they perform well there. Zero
TikTok-exclusive production until IG cadence has held for 6 weeks.

**LinkedIn — the Suor Society page, 2 posts/week.** (a) The week's best
carousel re-uploaded as a PDF document post — document posts get outsized
reach for pages; (b) one link post to a site article with a two-sentence
editorial intro. Voice stays Suor, lightly de-memed. The personal
build-in-public track on Thais's own profile is a separate project and not
part of this system's hours.

**The blog stays the asset.** Social points in; posts get repurposed out.
Nothing changes about the site workflow.

## Funnel — every post has a job

```
Reel/carousel reach → profile visit → bio link → Dispatch signup
                                   ↘ follow → weekly Dispatch-teaser post (T6)
```

- **CTA rotation** (one per post, never more): Save this → Send to your run
  partner → Follow for the culture → "The Dispatch goes deeper — link in bio."
  Roughly 2 soft : 1 follow : 1 Dispatch per week.
- **Bio link must be signup-first.** Today it points at a single article.
  Minimum fix: point it at `/dispatch`. Better fix: a `/links` page on the
  site (Dispatch form on top, latest 3 posts, race-guide PDFs below) — small
  build, can ship any time.
- **T6 (Dispatch teaser) runs every Friday** — the only post whose entire job
  is conversion.

## Measurement — the portfolio layer

Weekly scorecard (template in `weekly-system.md`): reach, profile visits,
follows, link taps, Dispatch signups, plus best/worst post and one lesson.
Filling it takes 10 minutes and produces two things at once: the monthly
double-down decision, and a receipts-included case study ("grew a bilingual
content brand from zero") for the personal-brand track later.

**90-day success looks like:** posting streak unbroken (the #1 metric),
reach trending up month over month, and Dispatch signups attributable to
social ≥ 2/week by week 12. Follower count is the vanity metric — watch it,
don't steer by it.
