"clean slate" reboot — same account, new system.

subscribers. The growth itself doubles as Thais's marketing portfolio.

## The one-paragraph version

Suor Society is an **editorial culture brand**, not a coach and not a news
wire. Every post is one of five pillars (a take on news, relatable hybrid
identity, race intel, gear verdicts, or pure brand/Brazil energy), produced in
one of three no-face formats (text-on-b-roll reels, designed carousels,
designed statics) using six recurring templates. Content ideas come from the
trend radar (already automated — candidates land on the Notion board daily)
plus the evergreen hook library. Everything is batched on Sunday, engagement
happens 15 minutes a day, and every post points at one destination: the
Dispatch. The weekly scorecard turns the whole thing into a public-ready
case study.

## Ground rules (from the first 3 posts' data)

1. **Identity beats information.** The brand-flag post (1,001 views) beat the
   news graphic (347) 3:1. News only ships wrapped in a take and a
   carousel/reel — never as a flat announcement card.
2. **Keep the account.** 2.6K views/30 days on 3 posts means the algorithm is
   already distributing. Clean slate = strategy, not handle.
3. **No format that requires dread.** Everything here is b-roll, voiceover,
   or designed templates. If a post idea needs a scripted talking head, it
   gets reformatted or cut.
