## T1 — The Take (carousel, 1080×1350, 4–6 slides)

The news format that replaces flat announcement graphics. Pillar 1.

- **Cover:** asphalt bg. Kicker top-left in JetBrains Mono, accent orange
  (`THE DISPATCH · JUL 2026`). Headline in Bebas, paper, huge (120–160pt),
  3–4 lines max — the headline IS the take, not the news
  ("THE MAJORS JUST BECAME SEVEN." not "Cape Town added as marathon major").
  Optional: photo strip or cutout on the right third, slightly desaturated.
- **Body slides (2–4):** bone bg, asphalt text. One idea per slide: Bebas
  sub-head (36–44pt) + 2–3 Inter sentences (~28pt). Orange underline under
  the sub-head. Mono counter bottom-right.
- **Last slide:** asphalt bg. The verdict in Bebas + one CTA line in mono
  ("FULL STORY ON SUORSOCIETY.COM → LINK IN BIO" or "SAVE THIS").
- **Headline formulas:** "X JUST HAPPENED. HERE'S WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGES." ·
  "THE SMARTEST DUMB ___ OF 2026." · "EVERYONE'S WRONG ABOUT ___." ·
  "___ IS THE NEW ___ (NO, REALLY)."
- **Caption skeleton:** 1-line restate of the take → 2–3 lines of context the
  slides didn't have → CTA → 3–5 niche hashtags (#hybridathlete #runningculture
  #hyrox + topic tags). By Suor Society, link in bio.

## T2 — The Hook Reel (video, 1080×1920, 7–15 s)

The sustainable video format: b-roll + one line of text. No face, no script,
optional voiceover. Pillars 1, 2, 4.

- **Construction:** 1–3 b-roll clips from the bank → hook text on screen for
  the full duration → quiet trending audio or ambient sound. Text style: IG's
  own text tool or CapCut — white text on subtle dark strip, or Bebas-style
  caps if using CapCut fonts. Hook appears within 0.5 s.
- **Voiceover variant (1–2×/month):** same reel, Thais reads the hook + one
  extra beat. The accent is an asset — it IS the brand story.
- **Caption carries the payoff:** the hook opens a loop on screen; the
  caption closes it in 2–4 sentences, then CTA.

## T3 — Race Intel (carousel, 1080×1350, 5–7 slides)

The save-magnet. Pillar 3. Feeds directly from `src/content/races-*.json`
and the existing guides — the content is already written.

- **Cover:** bone bg for contrast in-feed (this template is the light one).
  Mono kicker in slate (`RACE INTEL`), Bebas headline in asphalt
  ("40 RACES YOU CAN STILL RUN IN 2026"), orange tag chip ("NO LOTTERY.
  NO QUALIFYING.").
- **List slides:** 3–4 races per slide as rows: race name in Bebas (~40pt),
  city · date in JetBrains Mono below, thin slate divider between rows.
- **Last slide:** asphalt. "THE FULL GUIDE IS FREE" in Bebas + mono line
  "PDF + THE WEEKLY DISPATCH → LINK IN BIO". This is the template that
  converts — it links the lead magnet.
- **Caption:** who this list is for (1 line) → "Save this for registration
  season" → guide CTA. Dates in listings follow the same stale-date rule as
  the site — run `scripts/content/check-stale-dates.mjs` before posting a
  dated list.

## T4 — Gear Verdict (static or 3-slide carousel, 1080×1350)

Pillar 4. The anti-spec-sheet.

- **Layout:** product photo (press image or own shot) on asphalt, slightly
  desaturated. Bebas headline = the verdict, not the name ("THE PLATE IS
  GONE. GOOD."). Bottom third: slate panel with three mono rows —
  `WHO IT'S FOR:` / `WHO SHOULD SKIP:` / `THE CALL:` — each answered in one
  blunt Inter line.
- **Caption:** the two-sentence longer verdict → "Full drop breakdown on the
  site" when a dispatch post exists.

## T5 — Suor World (static, 1080×1350 or 1080×1080)

Pillar 5 — the proven performer (flag post: 1,001 views). Brand energy, zero
information.

- **Variant A — type poster:** asphalt bg, one massive Bebas statement
  ("SUOR. PORTUGUESE FOR SWEAT."), mono translation or footnote line under it
  in bone, orange accent mark. Bilingual pairs work here: big line in PT,
  small line in EN ("QUEM SUA, NÃO ENFERRUJA / who sweats doesn't rust").
- **Variant B — photo poster:** full-bleed San Diego / training / Brazil
  photo, wordmark large across it (flag energy), mono location-date stamp
  (`SAN DIEGO, CA · 32.7157°N`).
- **Caption:** short, confident, often one line. This template is allowed to
  say nothing useful. That's the job.

## T6 — Dispatch Teaser (carousel or static, 1080×1350) — every Friday

The only post whose entire job is email conversion.

- **Layout:** styled like a newspaper front. Mono kicker
  (`THE DISPATCH · ISSUE №__` ). Three headline rows from this week's
  issue in Bebas (mixed sizes, editorial hierarchy), each with a mono one-line
  sub. Bottom bar in orange: "ONE EMAIL A WEEK. THE LIFT-AND-RUN WORLD,
  EDITED DOWN. → LINK IN BIO" (this is the site's own copy — reuse it
  verbatim, it's good).
- **Caption:** tease the best item without resolving it → "The rest is in the
  Dispatch. Free, weekly, no spam. Link in bio."

## Cross-posting notes

- **TikTok:** reels re-upload without the IG watermark (export clean from
  CapCut). Carousels (T1/T3) post as TikTok photo posts — strong format
  there. Captions: shorter, hook first, more casual.
- **LinkedIn document posts:** export the week's best carousel from Canva as
  PDF, upload natively to the Suor page with a 3–4 line editorial intro.
  De-meme ~20%; keep the verdicts.
- **Stories:** raw and unpolished on purpose — b-roll with a sticker/poll,
  reshares of the day's post, countdowns. Zero design time budgeted.
