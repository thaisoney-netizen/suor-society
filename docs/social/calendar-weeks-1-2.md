## Week 3 onward

The training wheels come off: Sunday batch pulls from the trend radar + hook

plan in advance — that's the point of the system.

## What NOT to do in the first month

- No apology/absence posts. The reboot is silent — you just start.
- No follower-count screenshots or milestone posts until there's a real one.
- No new formats until these six templates have run for 4 weeks. Novelty is
  the enemy of the streak.
- No checking analytics before Friday. (Yes, this is in three documents now.
  It's the rule most worth repeating.)
