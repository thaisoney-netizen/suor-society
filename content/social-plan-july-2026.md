# Suor Society — Social Content Plan, July 2026

**Written:** July 3, 2026 · **Covers:** July 4–31, 2026 · **Primary platform:** Instagram (Reels + carousels + stories), with TikTok re-cuts optional.

This plan is built from (a) what Suor already is — hybrid run+lift culture for people with a real life, bilingual EN/pt-BR, San Diego + Brazil — and (b) what is measurably working for fitness/running brands right now (sources at the bottom).

---

## 1. What's working right now (July 2026) — and what it means for Suor

| Finding | What Suor does with it |

## 2. Content pillars (the 5 buckets — every post fits one)

1. **SWEAT SEASON (culture/motivation)** — heat training, effort culture, "your run counts the whole time." The emotional, shareable stuff. *Goal: shares + follows.*
2. **RACE PICKS (service)** — open-entry races worth entering, July 4th → fall block picks, Brazil winter races. *Goal: saves + guide downloads.*
3. **RUNNERS WHO LIFT (education/identity)** — lift days for runners, hybrid week structure, "levanta peso." *Goal: saves + niche authority.*
4. **THE DISPATCH IRL (news/takes)** — shoe drops, HYROX news, race news — the site's Dispatch, re-cut as takes. *Goal: comments + site traffic.*
5. **CREW / BEHIND THE SWEAT (community)** — San Diego crew, Thais's own training, real-life balance. *Goal: trust + DMs.*

**Rough mix per week:** 1 sweat-culture, 1 service (race/training), 1 runners-who-lift, 1 dispatch/news. Stories carry the crew/personal pillar daily.

## 5. Caption formula + hashtags

**Formula (keep captions short — the hook is on the image/video):**
1. Line 1 = re-state the hook differently (this is what shows in feed).
2. 2–3 lines of the take/context. Write like a text to a friend, not a brand.
3. One CTA (rotate: share / save / comment keyword / follow).
4. pt-BR echo line where relevant.

**Hashtags:** 3–5 max, niche > big. Core set: `#hybridathlete #runnerswholift #hyrox #runningcommunity #sweatsociety` · pt-BR: `#corridaderua #treinohibrido #hyroxbrasil`. Skip #fitness/#running (too broad to rank).

## 6. Growth mechanics (the stuff that compounds)

1. **Comment-to-DM funnel (do this first).** Set up ManyChat (free tier) or Instagram's native keyword auto-DM: comment **GUIDE** → DM the US race-guide landing page; **GUIA** → the Brazil guide. Every service carousel ends with this. This converts reach → emails, which is the actual asset.
2. **Optimize for sends.** Before posting anything ask: "who would someone send this to?" If the answer isn't obvious, sharpen the hook. Relatable pain ("training around a job") out-shares polished inspiration.
3. **First hour = engagement window.** Post, then spend 15 min replying to every comment and answering DMs. Comments are content.
4. **Stories do the daily work.** 1–2/day: training snippet, poll ("racing this fall?"), countdown stickers to HYROX SP / Anaheim, reshares of anyone tagging Suor. Zero production value required.
5. **Trend radar → Friday slot.** The morning digest (SUOR-8) exists exactly for this: each Friday post is the week's best candidate that passed the post-or-skip filter. If nothing passed, run the evergreen backup listed in the calendar.
6. **Cross-post to TikTok** only the Reels (Mon + Fri), 2–6pm Tue–Fri slots. Same file, native captions. No extra production.

## 7. Canva template system

You have Canva connected — either build these yourself with the steps below, or ask Claude to generate starter drafts in Canva and refine from there. Six templates cover the whole month; make them once, reuse forever.

### First: set up the Brand Kit (10 min, once)
Canva → Brand Hub → Brand Kit:
- **Colors:** Asphalt `#141414` · Paper `#FFFFFF` · Bone `#EDE8DC` · Slate `#2D3340` · Accent Orange `#E8750A`
- **Fonts (all free in Canva):** Display = **Bebas Neue** · Labels/eyebrows = **Barlow Condensed SemiBold** (all-caps, wide letter-spacing) · Body = **Inter** · Meta/dates = **JetBrains Mono**
- Upload the Suor wordmark/logo (export the site's SVG icon at `src/app/icon.svg` as PNG if you don't have one handy).

### The 6 templates

| # | Template | Size | Layout spec |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Carousel cover — "The Board"** | 1080×1350 | Bone background. Tiny Barlow eyebrow top-left ("SUOR SOCIETY / RACE PICKS"). Huge Bebas headline (3–4 words max, asphalt), one word in accent orange. JetBrains date bottom-left. Arrow "→" bottom-right. |
| 2 | **Carousel inner — list slide** | 1080×1350 | Paper background. Orange slide number top-right (Bebas, big). Barlow all-caps item title, Inter 2-line body. Thin asphalt divider line. Keep 60% white space. |
| 3 | **Carousel closer — CTA slide** | 1080×1350 | Asphalt background, bone text. Bebas: "COMMENT *GUIDE*" (GUIDE in orange). Inter subline: "and we'll DM you the full PDF." Logo bottom-center. |
| 4 | **Reel cover / hook card** | 1080×1920 | Full-bleed photo (your training footage frame), asphalt gradient bottom 40%, Bebas hook text in paper over it, orange underline accent under the key word. |
| 5 | **Quote / manifesto card** | 1080×1350 | Slate `#2D3340` background. Big Bebas quote in bone, quotation mark in orange. JetBrains attribution line. (For the "eight years" post and pt-BR proverbs.) |
| 6 | **Story countdown / callout** | 1080×1920 | Bone background, Bebas headline, native IG countdown sticker space reserved center, orange bar at bottom with Barlow "SUOR SOCIETY" repeated. |

### Build steps (per template, ~15 min each)
1. Create design → custom size above → name it `SUOR — [template name]` in a "Suor Templates" folder.
2. Lay out per the spec; use ONLY brand-kit colors/fonts (this is what makes the feed cohesive).
3. Set placeholder text to instructions ("HOOK GOES HERE, 4 WORDS MAX") so future-you moves fast.
4. Click Share → Template link (or just duplicate the file each week).
5. Weekly batching: Sunday, duplicate the week's 4 templates, drop in the calendar copy from §4, export all. ~45 min for the whole week's visuals.

## 8. Weekly workflow (fits around a real life — ~3h/week)

- **Sun (45–60 min):** read the week's calendar row, write 4 captions, fill templates, schedule via Meta Business Suite (free).
- **Mon–Fri (10 min/day):** post/confirm scheduled post, 1–2 stories, reply to comments in the first hour.
- **Fri (15 min):** pick next Friday's Dispatch topic from the trend-radar digests.
- **Sat:** shoot everything — crew run, gym, b-roll. One Saturday shoot feeds the whole week's Reels.

## 9. What to measure (monthly, 15 min)

- **Shares ("sends") per post** — the north-star signal. Double down on whatever gets sent.
- **Saves on carousels** — tells you which service content to make more of.
- **Keyword comments → guide downloads → Dispatch signups** — the funnel that actually matters.
- **Follower growth** — watch it, don't chase it.
