Color
Palette.

Bone is the dominant paper. Asphalt is the bold ink — used for type, strong overlays, and contrast moments. Slate supports sparingly. Sunrise, Highlight, and Rust are accent-only — a highlighted word, a dot, a tag. Never as background fills or dominant elements.

01 · Primary Bone Dominant. The paper of the brand. Default page background, default body surface.
02 · Secondary Asphalt Bold type, strong overlays, contrast bands. Used to anchor, never to dress.
03 · Supporting Slate Image card backs, pre-dawn moods. Sparingly. Never on its own as a section bg.
04 · Accents Sunrise · Hi · Rust Small moments only. A highlighted word, a dot, a border, a tag, a stamp.

Visual
Direction.

Three sentences. Treat this as the north star anytime a layout, a piece of imagery, or a component decision needs adjudicating.

N. STAR / 03 Suor Society is clean, warm, and editorial — Bone is the paper of the brand and Asphalt is the ink, with dark type on a warm light ground as the default state. Sunrise, Highlight, and Rust appear only as small moments — a highlighted word, a dot, a tag, a stamp — never as background fills or dominant elements. Corners are square, layouts are dense and grid-driven like a print magazine, motion is restrained (fades and small translates only, no springs, no parallax), and the system never reaches for gradients, drop shadows, or rounded "soft" containers to dress itself up.
